## BLOCKING (one batched fix round; designer tests first, then developer)
| Batch id | Source | Finding | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | RISK-1 / SD-1 | Approved-but-unfilled (in-flight) OPEN/ADD orders count toward no cap, margin, position count, direction check or Amendment 12 leverage. 4-5 simultaneous opens overcommit margin (400 on 300 equity; SOL risk 7.5 vs cap 4.5); a second entry on a coin with a pending entry picks a fresh lowest leverage, then merges at the first fill's leverage and breaks the liquidation rule. Fix: read-only broker query for pending entries (e.g. `PaperBroker.pending_entries()`, small F11 change) or a gate-side reservation released on fill/reject/cancel; count in free equity, position count, all caps, notional, direction; entry on a coin with a pending entry uses its leverage or is refused `entry_in_flight`. | designer + developer |
| B2 | SD-2 | Gate trusts the share book when broker and book disagree (broker share not in book: margin and stop missing from liquidation rule; fills from the gate's own advance_to exist in the broker before F12 consumed them). Fix: refuse entry `position_mismatch` when broker position share ids differ from the book's for that coin; sum margin over ALL broker positions (read-only `positions()` query); refuse/apply an entry when the submit's own advance_to produced entry fills. | designer + developer |
| B3 | RISK-2 / SD-3 | Add is sized and risk-recorded at its own tighter `stop_px`, but the share keeps `current_stop_px`. Real share risk 2-3x the cap. Fix: use the post-add share stop (`current_stop_px`) for caps, share cap, `initial_risk_usd` and the liquidation rule; tests with stop_px != current_stop_px. | designer + developer |
| B4 | RISK-3 / SD-6 | Loss limits fail open when marks stop; `mark_equity` trusts caller `now_ms` (one future mark rolls day/week and clears a halt). Fix: refuse entries before the first mark and when last mark older than 2 x eval.mark_interval_s (`equity_mark_stale`); bound `now_ms` against the gate's last exchange time + skew tolerance. | designer + developer |
| B5 | RISK-4 | Negative `open_risk_usd` (trailed stop past entry) loosens every cap. Fix: floor each share's open risk at 0 in every cap sum; document the F12 contract (risk >= 0). | designer + developer |
| B6 | RISK-5 / SD-8 | `/flatten` only walks the share book, does not pause, ignores in-flight entries. Fix: set the persisted manual pause first; close every share of every broker position; in-flight entries closed/blocked once they fill; outcome reports what is still in flight. | designer + developer |
| B7 | RISK-6 | CLOSE built before the gate's own advance_to can be stale (earlier ADD fills -> partial close; TP/stop partial -> `exceeds_position`). Fix: for `close=True`, net the quantity against fills for that share in `events` (or a per-share broker qty query) and close exactly what remains; a test for each direction. | designer + developer |
| B8 | SD-4 | Four test gaps: M12 exits count in the order-rate window; M23 re-sent exit refused `duplicate_order` before any token; M24 entry whose `risk_decision` append fails is refused with no token/broker call (inject at the OS boundary, not by mocking Ledger); M33 exit whose broker advance_to raises propagates. | designer |

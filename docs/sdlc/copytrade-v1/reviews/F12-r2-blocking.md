## RISK-43 (unfixed part of RISK-31; the designer's "ambiguity B"): flip leg fired by our own SL/TP/liquidation leaves the new share blind to the leader's exits
manager.py ~731-737 `_exit_filled`: the flip leg fires on ANY close of the share it waits for, including our own SL, TP or liquidation. It first runs `self._ours_won.add(key)`; the new share is opened by `_open_share` directly (not `_handle_open`), so `ours_won` is never cleared and every later exit of the leader on that coin is skipped as `first_exit_won`. Probe p6: our SL closes the long while the leader flips to short; the leg opens a short; the leader closes it 2 s later and that CLOSE is skipped; we still hold -1.00 SOL at +4 min with no order and no alert, until the 300 s reconcile.
Reviewer-risk's fix (one line): `self._ours_won.discard(key)` when the leg fires (the leg is the leader's new OPEN, as in `_handle_open`), or drop the leg on any close that is not our own flip close (the round-1 batch asked for the latter). Test: SL fills while the leg waits, then the leader closes: the new share is closed within one ack.
Loss: unmirrored short up to 300 s (longer if the reconcile read fails), up to its full stop (1R), more on a gap; most likely exactly when an SL and a flip coincide.

## RISK-44 (new hole in the RISK-33 heal; breaks A3 per-trade risk): `_heal_entry` uses the coin's MERGED average entry as this share's entry
`_heal_entry` takes `view.avg_entry_px` (merged average of ALL shares on the coin) as the healed share's entry and derives stop and TP from it. Probe p7 (test_p8_multi_share_avg): leader B's share at 100, our lost fill at 110: heal books entry 105, SL 103.5 (correct 108.5): stop 6.5 away instead of 1.5, recorded risk 15 USD vs true 65 USD; if the other share's entry is above ours the stop lands on the wrong side of the market and triggers at once.
Fix: use the share's own entry: (|view.qty| x avg - sum(qty x entry of the other OPEN shares in the book)) / qty, or fall back to `share.entry_px` (slippage-guarded decision price) when the coin holds other shares; then check the stop is on the correct side of the mark (else `_close_for_cause`). Test: heal with a second share on the coin at a different price.
Loss: two leaders hold SOL at 60 and 110; a lost fill healed at 85 with stop 83.5: 26.5 risk per unit vs a 1.5 budget (about 17x).

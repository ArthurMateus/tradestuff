Rulings: RISK-53, RISK-56, RISK-58 UPHELD. ROOT CAUSE: position management is gated on clock trust. Split: ENTRIES need a trusted exchange clock; EXITS need only a monotonic, forward-only, bounded broker time. The reviewers' design is sound with four adjustments:
1. Rebase needs TWO consecutive FRESH estimates (not N loop iterations: `exchange_now()` is local + offset and the offset changes only at a resample every 600 s). While in doubt force a resample every 30 s (add a method on ClockSync if needed; not a config key).
2. The jump guard compares the candidate with the MONOTONIC projection, not wall elapsed; symmetric (ahead or behind by more than the allowance = in doubt).
3. Startup baseline: after a restart with restored positions and an unsynced clock the baseline is the replayed broker time projected with monotonic time, flagged `unverified`; the first synced candidate is accepted without the jump guard (forward-only catch-up; funding charged correctly).
4. Broker time is forward-only: a backward rebase holds broker time flat and entries stay refused until the exchange estimate is back within the allowance.
Safety of a projection running ahead: bounded (monotonic drift only, ppm); no unconfirmed sample can move it (a +1 h sample is refused and accepted only after two fresh consistent estimates); exits stamped slightly ahead are paper fills; entries are refused while broker time is ahead so stale_decision/bad_decision_time cannot be bypassed; RISK-29 needs no extra cap. Marks do not depend on broker time.
## F11 amendment (Amendment 13, supersedes the 'not while the clock is unsynced' clause)
The supervisor calls `advance_to(broker_target)` on EVERY loop. `broker_target` is the last accepted exchange target plus monotonic elapsed time, forward-only. While the exchange clock is in doubt (unsynced, or a candidate outside the allowance of the projection), ENTRIES are refused (`clock_unsynced` / `clock_jump`). Exits, stops, trailing, liquidations, delistings, flatten and marks continue from the projection. RISK-29 now reads: the guard compares the candidate with the monotonic projection, not wall elapsed. Unchanged: entries outside [broker time, + filter.max_signal_age_ms] are refused, exits are clamped, advance_to is the only source of broker time.
## Behaviour spec (code constants, NO new config key)
DOUBT_RESAMPLE_S = 30; REBASE_FRESH_ESTIMATES = 2; DOUBT_REALERT_S = 300; MARKS_STALE_ALERT_N = 5 iterations; allowance = existing 2 x clock.max_offset_uncertainty_ms; monotonic source injected into TimeBase as `monotonic_ms: Callable[[], int]` defaulting to `time.monotonic_ns() // 1_000_000` (wall clock only for the offset estimate).
IN DOUBT = SyncedExchangeTime raises (unsynced/too uncertain/too old) OR |candidate - projection| > allowance OR the baseline is unverified and no synced sample has arrived yet.
EACH LOOP: projection = base_target + (mono_now - base_mono), forward-only; advance_to(projection), then _mark(projection), then _delistings; entries_ok = not in doubt (the gate sees ClockUnsyncedError from GuardedExchangeTime for entries when in doubt; exits keep the clamp).
REBASE: while in doubt force a resample every 30 s; rebase when two consecutive fresh synced estimates taken after the doubt began have implied offsets within the allowance of each other; then base_target = max(projection, candidate) (broker time never goes back; a forward rebase jumps once and funding is charged normally); alert `clock_rebased` once.
ALERTS: entering doubt: one `clock_unsynced`/`clock_jump`; while in doubt AND broker.positions() non-empty: re-alert every 300 s ("clock in doubt, entries refused, N open positions managed from projection"); no repeats without positions; `clock_rebased` once per rebase; reset alert state on rebase/recovery.
/FLATTEN IN DOUBT: runs normally (closes are exits driven by the projection). The flatten supervisor treats the flatten as UNFINISHED while broker.positions() is non-empty OR pending exits (exit/stop orders awaiting fill) are non-empty OR in_flight/still_open is non-empty; re-run at the existing interval; at the existing limit send `flatten_incomplete` once then every 300 s while unfinished; done only when everything is empty; a raise in manager.flatten is caught and logged, the flatten stays unfinished and a re-run is still scheduled (SD13).
MARKS ALERT (RISK-57): count consecutive iterations with positions open and stale mids, or a rejected/unknown mark; at 5 send `marks_stalled`; repeat every 300 s while it lasts; reset on a good mark.

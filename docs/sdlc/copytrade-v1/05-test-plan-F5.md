## Fixtures and where the numbers come from

- **Reconstruction vector:** `hl_sample.py` self-test 1, copied fill for fill (Decimal). Expected values are the asserts in that self-test.
- **H1** (`wallets.py::h1`): 7 closed round trips over BTC and ETH, one open SOL trip, a pre-existing ETH position, one HIP-3 and one spot fill, a flip, an add-while-losing, a partial. Cycle time day 300, window 90 days. Every trip's P&L, hold, notional and account value is written in the comments. The expected values were computed with an independent script (standard library only, not the code under test) and cross-checked by hand for the round-number metrics (n_rt 7, span 79 d, age 200 d, median hold 120 min, PF 152.7/112.8, maker 3960/24109, drawdowns 300/10100, executable 6/7, leverage median 0.1).
- **W_R:** four open/close trips on flat 1h candles (ATR 2 at price 100, stop 4%), zero costs. R = +0.5, -0.5, +0.25, -1 (stop hit first on a bar that also reaches the 2R target). Mean -0.1875.
- **W_M:** 90 days with 10 own-hourly days, 20 perpMonth days and 60 fill days, so the source priority and the resolution counts are exercised, and perpAllTime points are added at random to prove they are ignored.
- **DSR and SR0 literals:** `statistics.NormalDist`, gamma 0.5772.
- **Score vector V1:** hand computed in the `test_score.py` docstring; S = 0.60.
- **Healthy wallets:** 80 daily round trips, alternating BTC and ETH. Eligible under the permissive config (window 90, `gate.min_round_trips` 50, `gate.min_positive_blocks` 1, `gate.max_top_asset_share` 0.6). Their DSR is 0.99+ (checked with the same script). They are used for pipeline properties, never for numeric S literals.

## Decisions taken where the spec is silent (test-designer; the PM or PO should confirm)

1. **Numeric type of ratios.** All gate-compared ratios are `Decimal`, not `float`: a float `0.95` is below `Decimal("0.95")`, so an exact boundary test on DSR would fail for the wrong reason.
2. **Statistics definitions.** `sr_d = mean / sd` with the sample sd (n-1). `skew` and `kurt` use population central moments (`m3/m2^1.5`, `m4/m2^2`, non-excess), as in the DSR paper. The spec says "sample skewness" without pinning either. Recent SR is over days `[t-30d, t)`.
3. **Daily-return days.** Every complete UTC day in `[t - window_days, t)` that has an account-value point at or before the end of the previous day is a day; days with none are excluded (BT-3) and not counted in T. Sources per day: own hourly, else perpMonth (covering the whole day), else realised P&L from all core-perp fills (zero if none, so "zeros included"). `perpAllTime` P&L is never used for a return. `dsr_resolution` is the count of days per source.
4. **Blocks (M8).** Block i covers `[t - i*block_days, t - (i-1)*block_days)`; its sum is over the daily P&L above; a block with no day counts as not positive.
5. **M14 copy replay.** Tested only for open/close trips: entry at the open price plus half spread plus delay adverse, exit at the leader's close (or our stop trigger, stop first on an ambiguous bar), taker fee on both legs, R = return / stop distance, stop = 2 x ATR(14) on 1h bars closed before entry over the entry price. Take-profit (2R half close), the trail, mirrored adds and partials inside the replay are not pinned by the spec and are not tested; they depend on F12's exit rules. Costs come through a `CostModel` protocol (taker, half spread, delay per coin), because the spec names the cost terms (M15) but not their source. No candles gives `copy_mean_r = None`, so G10 fails.
6. **M16 and M19 population.** Closed round trips only. Opens with no account value at or before them are excluded from numerator and denominator. Executable means `min(open_notional / AV x paper.wallet_usd, risk.per_trade_fraction x wallet / stop_pct) >= sizing.min_order_usd`.
7. **BU8** uses net unrealised loss of all open positions in `clearinghouseState` divided by its account value. BU2 orders trips by open time and looks at the previous trip's sign.
8. **Latency.** G8 fails closed when the p95 latency is unknown (`None`). The cycle takes it as an argument.
9. **Reason codes.** `stale_input` (from the spec), `G1`..`G15`, `BU1`..`BU8`; sorted.
10. **Duplicate fills** (same `tid`) count once inside F5 even if F3 already dedupes.
11. **Persistence** is through a `ScoreStore.append_cycle(CycleResult)` protocol; F2's ledger adapts to it. Input hashes are sha256 over the point-in-time inputs actually used, per kind, and exclude the leaderboard row.
12. **Fill type.** F5 defines its own `Fill`, `Candle`, `PortfolioSnapshot` and `ClearinghouseState` in `models.py` with HL wire names; F3's adapter must map to them. No recorded real payload exists under `tests/fixtures/exchange/`; the wire-format contract belongs to F3's tests.

## Spec / F1 issues to resolve

1. **F1 config loader rejects weights that sum to 1 within 1e-9.** F5.AC5 says "± 1e-9"; `core/config.py` does `sum(weights) != 1`. `test_F5_AC5_weights_within_1e_9_of_one_load` fails on an F1 assertion, not on a stub. The fix is one line in a shared file, so it needs a CTO-serialized commit before or with the F5 build. Alternatively the PM drops the tolerance.
2. **F5.AC4's example is arithmetically impossible.** "At 10 adds it fires at a share of 0.21 but not at 0.20": with exactly 10 adds, shares are multiples of 0.1. The tests use 10 adds at 0.20 (no) and 0.30 (yes), and 100 adds at 0.21 (yes) and 0.20 (no).
3. **Funding.** Section 10.1 subtracts `funding_j` from the round-trip P&L, but the fills interface has no funding. The tests give F5 a separate funding list (`userFunding`, F3). If F3 does not provide it, the spec must say funding is excluded.
4. **Cost source for M14 and M15.** The spec gives the cost terms (taker, half spread, delay) but not where per-coin recorded values come from at scoring time (F4 recordings or the config fallbacks in §3.7). The tests inject them through a protocol. The developer or PM should confirm the wiring, and that the tier (major or alt) rule applies.
5. **M14 depends on F12's exit rules** (TP at 2R for half, trail after +1R). F5 must either share that code or freeze a copy; either way that leg has no F5 test yet.
6. **Skew, kurtosis and sd conventions** (decision 2) should be confirmed by the quant-researcher.
